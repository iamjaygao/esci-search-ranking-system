## 5. Conclusion

Both benchmarks are internally valid and both remain usable for internal experiments on their own populations. Only the NEW benchmark corresponds to the KDD Cup 2022 Task 1 data definition and may be used as the reference for Task 1 work.

The OLD benchmark (`experiments/ranking_v2/official_metric_final/`) is **retained** as the LARGE-VERSION INTERNAL RANKING BENCHMARK. Its conclusions still hold on its own population. It must not be deleted, and its numbers must never be compared with, subtracted from, or quoted alongside Task 1 numbers — see the bookkeeping rule in section 16 of the REPORT.

Absolute NDCG on the NEW benchmark is expected to be lower than on the OLD one. That is a consequence of the benchmark definition changing (harder query subset, heavier JP weight, full-list rather than @10), **not** a regression and not a bug.
